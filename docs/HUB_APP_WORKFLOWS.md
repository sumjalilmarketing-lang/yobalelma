# Hub App Workflows

## Inbound Reception

The Hub can scan expected, missing, damaged, quarantined and extra packages. Discrepancies require a note. Scans update shipment state, inventory, tracking events, notifications and audit logs.

## Inspection

Agents record measured weight, packaging quality, a human decision and comments. Weight tolerance rules recommend blocking or confirmation, but irreversible decisions remain human-submitted.

## Storage And Inventory

Inventory items have a single active location. Movements update location, status, tracking and audit logs.

## Capacity And Batches

Managers can create batches from validated trips, reserve compatible shipments and prevent active double assignment. Capacity checks enforce destination and remaining weight.

## Pickup QR And Handover

Ready batches generate opaque QR tokens linked to batch, hub, traveler and trip. Handover verifies token, identity, document and ticket before marking shipments handed over and invalidating the token.

## Anomalies

Agents create and resolve anomalies with priority, blocking flags and audit logs.
